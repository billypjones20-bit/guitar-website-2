Fender Stratocaster

The Fender Stratocaster, often referred to as the "Strat", is one of the most iconic and influential electric guitars in history. Introduced by Fender in 1954, it has been used by countless legendary musicians across all genres.

div>

Key Features:

  • Triple single-coil pickups
  • 6-saddle synchronized bridge
  • 22 frets on maple or rosewood fingerboard
  • Contoured body for comfort
  • Three-way pickup selector switch
  • Volume and two tone controls
div>

History

Designed by Leo Fender, George Fullerton, and Freddie Tavares, the Stratocaster was created to address limitations of the earlier Telecaster. Its innovative design included a contoured body, tremolo system, and three pickups.

div>

Famous Users

Eric Clapton, Jimi Hendrix, David Gilmour, Stevie Ray Vaughan, Jeff Beck, and many more have made the Stratocaster their signature instrument.
