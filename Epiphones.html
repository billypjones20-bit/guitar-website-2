Epiphone Guitars

Epiphone Les Paul Standard

The Epiphone Les Paul Standard is a premium semi-hollow body electric guitar that offers exceptional tone and playability. Known for its rich, warm sound and classic design, it's a favorite among rock and blues musicians.

Key Features:

  • Alnico Classic Pro humbucking pickups
  • Maple top with mahogany body
  • 24.75" scale length
  • Rolling vintage tone controls

Epiphone SG Standard

The Epiphone SG Standard is a lightweight, comfortable electric guitar with a distinctive double-cutaway design. Famous for its bright, cutting tone and excellent sustain, it's perfect for rock, punk, and alternative music.

Key Features:

  • ProBucker pickups for versatile tones
  • Mahogany body with maple top
  • Fast Profile neck for comfortable playing
  • Lightweight design

Epiphone Casino

The Epiphone Casino is an iconic semi-hollow body guitar famous for its use by The Beatles, The Who, and many other legendary artists. It delivers a warm, resonant tone with excellent acoustic properties.

Key Features:

  • Alnico Pro humbucking pickups
  • Semi-hollow maple body construction
  • Double cutaway design
  • Classic trapeze tailpiece
